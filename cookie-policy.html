Flight Ticket Booking Application - Cookie Policy:
  1. This Cookie Policy explains how IndiFly365 ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our Flight Ticket Booking Application (the "Application"). By using our Application, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

  2. What Are Cookies?

    • Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website or use an application. Cookies allow the website or application to recognize your device and store information about your preferences or actions.

  3. How We Use Cookies

    • Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the basic functionality of our Application. They enable you to navigate our website and use its features, such as accessing secure areas and making flight bookings. Without these cookies, our Application may not function properly.
    • Performance Cookies: These cookies collect anonymous information about how you use our Application, such as which pages you visit and any errors you may encounter. We use this information to improve the performance and functionality of our Application.
    • Functional Cookies: These cookies allow our Application to remember choices you make (such as your username, language, or region) and provide enhanced features. These cookies may also be used to personalize your experience.
    • Third-Party Cookies: Some cookies may be placed on your device by third-party services we use, such as analytics services and social media platforms. These cookies are subject to the privacy policies of the third parties providing them.

  4. Managing Cookies

    • Cookie Preferences: You can manage your cookie preferences in the Application's settings or in your browser's settings. You can usually block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
    • Disabling Cookies: Please note that if you disable cookies, some features of our Application may not function correctly, and your ability to make bookings may be affected.

  5. Changes to this Cookie Policy

    • We may update this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of any material changes via the Application. Continued use of the Application after the effective date of changes constitutes acceptance of the modified policy.

  6. Contact Us

    • If you have any questions or concerns about this Cookie Policy, please contact us at support@indifly365.com.
